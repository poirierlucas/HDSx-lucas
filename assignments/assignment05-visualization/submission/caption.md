- **Statistic and source:** Mean BMI (kg/m²) with unweighted 95% intervals
  (mean ± 1.96 SE) for adults age 20+, by family poverty-income ratio group,
  across NHANES cycles 1999-2000 to 2021-2023. The data are the CDC/NCHS
  NHANES public-use classroom snapshot (`nhanes_equity_v6`, n = 51,892 adults).
- **Exclusions:** Children and teens under 20 were excluded, along with 6,570
  adults missing BMI or income group. NHANES did not collect a 2019-2020
  cycle, so the line is broken there.
- **Limitations:** The results are descriptive and unweighted (survey weights,
  strata, and PSUs are not used). They are not U.S. population estimates and
  do not show that income causes BMI. The y-axis starts at 25, not 0.
