# Provenance and Stewardship Note

- **Data source:** The analysis uses the NHANES Health Equity classroom snapshot `nhanes_equity_v6.csv`, a prepared file of 105626 participant records from NHANES cycles 1999-2000 to 2021-2023.
- **Source of the source:** The snapshot was prepared by the course from public-use NHANES files published by the CDC's National Center for Health Statistics.
- **Access method:** The file was not downloaded from the CDC website for this analysis. It is a cached copy read with a relative path from the course repository, `examples/nhanes-equity/data/nhanes_equity_v6.csv`.
- **Classroom-use boundary:** The data are used for descriptive classroom learning only. Summaries are unweighted, with no survey weights, strata, or PSUs, so they describe the prepared file and are not U.S. population estimates or causal effects.
- **Privacy caution:** Although the files are public-use and de-identified, row-level records are not uploaded to external AI tools or shared outside the course, and results are reported as group summaries rather than small-cell or individual-level claims that could help re-identify someone.
